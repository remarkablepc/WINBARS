ScamBuster lockup simulator

Shows what ScamBuster does when a fake "virus alert" page takes over the screen. Nothing here is real: the phone number is fictional and nothing is sent or installed.

Before you start (Mode 4)

  1. The Floppy Tray Sentry must be running (it checks the screen about every 2.5 seconds).
  2. Close anything you care about: choosing RECLAIM MY COMPUTER closes all browser windows.

What you should see

  1. The page goes full screen with a flashing red "virus" alert and a countdown.
  2. Within a few seconds ScamBuster mutes browser audio and shows the shop-branded rescue dialog: "THE SCREEN SHOWING BEHIND THIS PROMPT IS A SCAM".
  3. Pick Cancel / Keep Open to keep demonstrating, or RECLAIM MY COMPUTER to show the clean-up.
Exit at any time with Esc, or click End demo on the black bar. The demo also ends by itself after 3 minutes.
